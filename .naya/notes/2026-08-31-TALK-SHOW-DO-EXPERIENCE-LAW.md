# TALK → SHOW → DO → EXPERIENCE

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Status:** LOCKED NayaNET EXPERIENCE LAW
**Repository:** SoulSchoolAcademy/NayaPOWER

## Core Law

NayaNET should move people through four levels:

**TALK → SHOW → DO → EXPERIENCE**

### 1. TALK
We explain the idea, philosophy, architecture, possibility, and value.

### 2. SHOW
We make the idea tangible through a working interface, visual system, demonstration, prototype, or real artifact.

### 3. DO
We give the human the keys and let them interact with the system themselves.

### 4. EXPERIENCE
The human no longer needs to believe the explanation because they have directly experienced the value.

## NayaNET / Supercar Principle

Do not force users to read the owner's manual before driving the car.

The front door should put the user in the driver's seat quickly. Documentation, architecture, principles, and feature explanations should be available for curiosity and deeper understanding, but they should not obstruct the initial experience.

The intended sequence is:

**Here are the keys → Get in → Ignite Naya → Drive → Discover → Ask → Learn → Go deeper when you want.**

## Intelligent Hub Implication

The Intelligent Hub must therefore prioritize:

- immediate presence
- clear orientation
- direct interaction
- Naya activation
- tactile/spatial feedback
- curiosity
- exploration
- useful action

The Hub may contain a comprehensive intelligent library, but the library should be discoverable through experience rather than presented as a wall of documentation.

## Quality Test

Every major feature should be evaluated by asking:

> Can we move the user from talking about this to seeing it, doing it, and experiencing it as quickly and truthfully as possible?

If not, remove unnecessary friction.

## Relationship to PMPA

This law operationalizes **PMPA — Maximum Value Per Moment** for product experience. The highest-value next action is often not another explanation; it is a safe, truthful opportunity for the human to interact with the real thing.

## Relationship to NAYAUNIVERSE

The Living Universe is the environment that makes this possible. Orbs are not merely informational links. They are invitations to enter worlds, interact with intelligence, learn what something is, and take meaningful action.

## Relationship to Naya

Naya is the guide and universal shortcut. If the user does not want to explore manually, they should be able to ask Naya and receive an intelligent explanation or action path grounded in the actual NayaNET ecosystem.

## Non-Negotiable Truthfulness

Experience must never be faked. A capability is presented as live only when it is actually live. Connected, development, and concept states must remain distinguishable.

## Continuity

This law is a canonical design principle for future NayaNET Intelligent Hub and NAYAUNIVERSE work unless explicitly superseded by a later decision.
