## Important Design Correction

The first E01 visual prototype revealed that a Living Sun can become decorative rather than meaningful. The Living Sun must therefore have an intentional experiential purpose when used. Naya presence should be the emotional anchor; the Living Sun should communicate meaningful system state rather than exist merely as an impressive-looking orb.

## Locked E01 Direction

E01 is the **Door to NayaNET**:

`Welcome → Naya presence → free identity → Smart Name / Smart Link → Intelligent Hub → next action`

The opening experience should minimize friction, use the official Naya brand assets, avoid fake functionality, and make the next action obvious.
