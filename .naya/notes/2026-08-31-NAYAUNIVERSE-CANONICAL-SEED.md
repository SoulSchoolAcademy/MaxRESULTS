## Purpose

`NAYAUNIVERSE` is the canonical seed/reference for the Living Intelligence Universe direction of the NayaNET Intelligent Hub.

It captures the originating architecture: Naya as the Living Sun; a growing universe of intelligence objects; center-stage transformation when an object is selected; and a spatial expansion that may evolve from rings into a continuous spiral / Fibonacci-inspired flow.

## Canonical Architecture

- Naya is the central Living Sun.
- The Hub is a Living Intelligence Library / Universe, not a conventional dashboard.
- The final number of orbs is intentionally unknown and must emerge from a complete ecosystem inventory.
- Objects may flow outward from the center in rings, orbital planes, or a continuous spiral.
- Fibonacci / golden-ratio / seashell / toroidal imagery is a design inspiration for organic expansion, not a rigid mathematical constraint.
- Selecting an object makes it the center-stage world while the surrounding universe reorganizes around it.
- Every object can explain what it is, why it matters, how it works, what the user can do, how it connects, and its truthful status.
- Naya can explain or navigate any object conversationally.

## Experience Principle

The Hub should put people in the driver's seat. They should be able to experience Naya immediately, then explore the universe or ask Naya to explain it.
