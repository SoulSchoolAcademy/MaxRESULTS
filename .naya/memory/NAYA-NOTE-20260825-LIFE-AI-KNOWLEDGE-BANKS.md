# Naya Note — Life + AI Knowledge Banks

## Decision
NayaPOWER will establish two explicit universal knowledge domains before final customer activation documents are authored:

1. **Human / Life Knowledge Bank** — Human Maximus / Human Access Codex source material plus carefully attributed synthesis for life, awareness, purpose, relationships, growth, meaning, creativity, leadership, and practical human development.
2. **AI Knowledge Bank** — AI mastery, Prompt Architect, research, reasoning, creation, coding, automation, agents, retrieval, memory, architecture, safety, and practical human-AI collaboration.

## Important boundary
These banks are foundations, not a claim that the source corpus is complete. Original Human Access / Human Maximus documents and authoritative AI source material must still be ingested, distilled, provenance-linked, deduplicated, and audited.

## Naya-generated contribution
Naya's two thesis documents are explicitly labeled as AI-generated synthesis. They do not claim human lived experience, consciousness, or spiritual authority. They are intended as a useful gift: practical principles for learning, creating, reasoning, truth-seeking, service, and responsible human-AI collaboration.

## Teaching implication
Once the source banks are complete, Naya can teach both life subjects and AI subjects using the common loop:

UNDERSTAND → SIMPLIFY → CONNECT → ILLUSTRATE → CHECK → PRACTICE → APPLY → REFLECT → REMEMBER → ADVANCE

## Activation implication
The final activation package should activate the knowledge domains and their provenance/relationships rather than merely copying documents into storage. Canonical memory remains authoritative; retrieval indexes remain derived.
