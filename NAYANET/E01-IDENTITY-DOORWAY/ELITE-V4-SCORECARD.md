# NayaNET E01 — Elite v4 Scorecard

## Decision
v4 is the corrective design pass after reviewing the existing NayaNET doorway against the canonical NayaNET World, Five-Day Challenge, MAXESS experience language, and the user's latest visual direction.

## What v4 corrects

- Rich black is the dominant material; purple is the primary energy/accent color.
- Lime is reserved for the name field's idle state and privacy/status signals rather than becoming the page's primary brand color.
- The doorway is an app-like centered surface rather than a conventional landing page.
- The NayaNET icon is centered in the doorway rather than placed as a side-navigation brand mark.
- The doorway uses a bright, readable white boundary with a restrained purple atmospheric halo.
- The headline uses a more editorial serif treatment to avoid the flat generic SaaS appearance.
- The supporting line is treated as a quotation-like invitation rather than a dashboard subtitle.
- "Free to enter · No email required" is intentionally readable instead of disappearing into black-on-black microcopy.
- Enter NayaNET is white-bordered at rest and transitions into purple energy when valid/active.
- Buttons use layered black surfaces, physical depth, underglow, hover elevation, press compression, and focus states.
- Subsequent screens slide as app states rather than behaving like unrelated web pages.

## Experience contract

`Door → Identity → Reveal → Intelligent Hub → Naya Power`

The user should feel that they have entered one intelligent application and are being guided through a sequence of meaningful steps.

## Truth contract

- Smart Link remains explicitly PREVIEW.
- Identity is session-local in this review artifact.
- Production persistence is not claimed.
- Smart Mail is not claimed.
- PSI ingestion is not claimed.
- MAXESS is marked Coming Next.
- The supplied Naya Power introduction video is connected.
- Individual lesson-video URLs are not invented.
