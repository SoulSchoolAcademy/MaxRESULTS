## Why the button system matters

The primary control is treated as a product signature: rich black, physical depth, compressed press state, restrained purple underglow, lime active energy, precise focus state, and subtle motion. The goal is that users remember **how NayaNET feels to use**, not merely how it looks.

## Remaining gap before production

The visual/interaction doorway can be reviewed as a complete experience, but production identity persistence and provisioning are not yet connected. MAXESS remains a real future handoff rather than a fake screen. Individual challenge lesson videos remain unverified.

**Verdict:** review-ready experience build; not a claim of production backend completeness.
