## Oscar attack list

1. **Generic SaaS?** No — deep-space composition, living Naya orb, dimensional controls, sparse hierarchy.
2. **Too much UI?** No — one job per room; Hub remains deliberately minimal.
3. **Fake capability?** No — production boundaries are visible in the UI and README.
4. **Real-name leakage?** Downstream views use Smart Name only.
5. **Broken media claim?** Only the supplied introduction video is embedded; individual lesson media is not fabricated.
6. **Social-media drift?** Explicitly excluded.
7. **Motion overload?** Motion is restrained and disabled/reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
8. **Mobile collapse?** Grid and action layouts recompose at 820px and 560px breakpoints.
9. **Accessibility cliff?** Inputs/buttons are labelled and keyboard-operable; state messages use live regions.
10. **Architecture drift?** E01 is an experience layer; no competing identity, memory, feed, PSI or communication system is introduced.
