# Human Maximus / MAXESS — AAA Button & Icon System

Status: AUTHORITATIVE WORKING DESIGN SYSTEM
Scope: Human Maximus Codex, MAXESS, Naya, NayaNET and related digital products
Purpose: Create a recognizable, premium, high-conviction interaction language that feels alive, tactile, intelligent and unmistakably Human Maximus.

## North Star
Controls are not decoration. A button is a moment of commitment. An icon is a moment of recognition. A bullet/icon marker is a moment of rhythm.

Every control must make the user understand: WHAT IS THIS? WHY SHOULD I CARE? WHAT HAPPENS IF I PRESS IT?

The control system must feel premium, human, futuristic, warm, confident, cinematic, clear, responsive, tactile and memorable.

## AAA Control Principle
Use CLARITY + HIERARCHY + DEPTH + MOTION + FEEDBACK + PURPOSE.

A control is AAA when its purpose is immediately understandable; visual importance matches UX importance; tactile depth is believable; hover/focus/pressed/disabled states are clear; it works on touch, mouse and keyboard; contrast is strong; motion communicates state; it belongs to the surrounding visual system; it is recognizable at a glance; and it feels proprietary rather than default.

## Human Maximus Visual Language
Core palette: BLACK for authority/depth, WHITE for clarity/breathing room, PURPLE for intelligence/transformation/Naya identity, GOLD for premium achievement moments.

Prefer deep contrast, selective luminous edges, layered surfaces, soft radial energy, restrained gradients, premium typography, generous whitespace, rounded/organic geometry and high-quality icons.

Avoid excessive glassmorphism, noisy neon, giant shadows and effects that reduce readability.

## Button Families

### B1 — Primary Action
One dominant action per major view when possible.
Examples: START YOUR FREE TRIAL, GET MY AI SCORE, CONTINUE WITH NAYA, LISTEN TO NAYA.
Use high contrast, strong pill or soft-round geometry, dimensional surface, restrained gradient/energy treatment, optional contextual icon, hover lift and tactile press compression.

### B2 — Secondary Action
Examples: LEARN MORE, EXPLORE, SEE MY DIMENSIONS, WATCH THE VIDEO.
Lower visual weight than B1 while retaining the same geometry, typography and icon family.

### B3 — Context Action
Examples: VIEW, OPEN, MORE, DETAILS. Keep restrained and never let it compete with B1.

### B4 — Listen to Naya
Premium audio action using waveform/sound identity, playing/paused state, subtle pulse, Naya purple and optional Orb synchronization. Major report experiences should have one clearly dominant Listen action.

### B5 — Pathway Action
Examples: OPEN MASTER, START THIS PATH, EXPLORE THIS DOOR. Compact but premium, clearly related to its pathway.

### B6 — Commitment / Conversion
Examples: JOIN FREE, START MY JOURNEY, TAKE YOUR POWER BACK, CONTINUE WITH NAYANET. Confident, emotionally aligned and visually decisive.

## Button Copy
Prefer verb + benefit, verb + destination, or verb + outcome. Avoid vague labels such as CLICK HERE, SUBMIT, GO, NEXT or OKAY unless context makes the meaning unambiguous.

## Geometry and Depth
Preferred forms: pill, rounded capsule, soft rounded rectangle, occasional circular icon-only control. Use geometry to communicate hierarchy rather than making every control identical.

AAA depth is layered but believable: base surface → subtle border → soft highlight → restrained shadow → hover lift → pressed compression.

## Required States
REST, HOVER, FOCUS, PRESSED, DISABLED, LOADING, and SUCCESS when meaningful. Focus must be visible without relying on mouse hover.

## Icon System
Icons are a coherent visual language, not stickers. Use one family with consistent stroke weight, corners, optical sizing and baseline alignment.

Suggested semantic mapping:
LISTEN = waveform/sound; PLAY = play triangle/capsule; EXPLORE = compass/spark/direction; OPEN = arrow/chevron; SUCCESS = check/radiant mark; INFO = subtle information circle; AI = spark/intelligence glyph; PATHWAY = door/route; INSIGHT = eye/lens/orb; ACTION = directional arrow/energy mark.

## Icon-only Controls
Every icon-only button needs an accessible name, useful tooltip/context where appropriate, practical mobile touch target, visible focus and predictable behavior. Never depend on icon recognition alone where ambiguity is possible.

## Bullets and Micro-icons
Use small luminous dots, checks, arrows, miniature orbs, sparks, pathway markers or subtle geometric glyphs. They should create rhythm and hierarchy rather than noise.

## Typography
Controls must be highly legible, confident, compact and consistent with the broader brand type system. Uppercase micro-label styling can work for major CTAs. Avoid decorative fonts for core actions.

## Motion
Use motion for availability, feedback, transition, energy and progress. Favor subtle 120–220ms responses, lift, press compression, soft glow, Listen pulse and restrained entrance animation. Always support reduced motion.

## Responsive Rules
Desktop: stronger hierarchy and generous spacing. Tablet: preserve hierarchy and reduce padding before legibility. Mobile: prioritize clarity, touchability and readable labels; stack actions when needed; avoid tiny icon-only controls.

## Accessibility
Use semantic buttons/links, visible focus, sufficient contrast, accessible names, color-independent state communication, reduced-motion behavior and touch-friendly sizing.

## Quality Test
Before shipping a button ask: Is its purpose immediate? Does it look expensive? Does it feel tactile? Does it clearly belong to Human Maximus? Is hierarchy obvious? Is the action emotionally aligned? Does it work on touch and keyboard? Does it provide clear feedback? Does it remain excellent in real surrounding content?

If not: SCORE → IDENTIFY THE GAP → IMPROVE → VERIFY → FREEZE.

## Product-wide Rule
Once a button/icon family is approved, reuse it across the product. Do not invent a new control style for every section. A strong product feels like one intelligent system.

## Highest Standard
The bar is not “better than ordinary websites.” The bar is: WOULD I BE PROUD TO SHOW THIS CONTROL BY ITSELF AS AN EXAMPLE OF HUMAN MAXIMUS DESIGN?
