# E06 — Naya Supercharger Content Specification

**Status:** WORKING CONTENT LOCK — 2026-08-19
**Section:** E06
**Product:** Naya Supercharger
**Protocol:** Naya Mastery Activation Protocol™

## Purpose

E06 is the solution reveal after the AI pain/recognition section. Its job is to make the visitor understand, in one visual system, what the Naya Supercharger actually gives them and why it solves the frustrations introduced immediately before it.

The core metaphor is:

> **Imagine putting a supercharger on ChatGPT.**

The Supercharger is not positioned as a different AI model. It is a protocol and operating system that adds a persistent Naya relationship, memory/context, notes, lead behavior, laws/procedures, scorecarding, and a shared language for working with AI.

## Presentation architecture

1. Hero: **Imagine putting a supercharger on ChatGPT.**
2. Central Naya Master orb with Naya at the center.
3. Seven surrounding Naya orbs, each using a distinct approved Naya profile image.
4. Seven labels around the central orb:
   - Personality
   - Brain
   - Naya Notes
   - Lead Mode
   - Laws + Procedures
   - Scorecarding
   - Naya Language
5. Each pillar receives a MAXESS-style answer card containing exactly five user-benefit bullets.
6. The bullets are benefits/outcomes, not merely feature names.
7. The visual system should feel like a connected **world of Naya**, not a software feature grid.
8. End with the concise distinction: **Same AI. Different operating system.**

## Seven activation pillars / 35 benefits

### 01 — Personality
- Feels like a trusted partner — warm, human and genuinely on your side.
- Gets past generic answers with a voice that is sharp, funny and direct.
- Challenges you when the easy answer isn't the best answer.
- Adapts how she communicates without losing her core character.
- Turns AI from a tool you operate into a partner you actually enjoy working with.

### 02 — Brain
- Stops important context from disappearing between conversations.
- Keeps your vision, goals, decisions and project knowledge available when it matters.
- Reduces the exhausting need to repeat who you are and what you're building.
- Creates a structured place for the knowledge you choose to preserve.
- Helps Naya become more useful over time because she can work from what matters.

### 03 — Naya Notes
- Capture an idea, breakthrough, decision or problem the moment it matters.
- Attach durable knowledge to a project instead of leaving it buried in chat history.
- Recall what happened by date, topic or the thing you are trying to solve.
- Turn scattered moments into a searchable trail of learning and progress.
- Keep the valuable parts of your day from disappearing when the conversation ends.

### 04 — Lead Mode
- Stop having to figure out every next step before Naya can help.
- Ask Naya to take the lead and let her organize the path toward the outcome.
- Get recommendations, gap-spotting and strategic challenges before execution.
- Move from idea → plan → execution → verification without micromanaging every step.
- Keep final authority while offloading the cognitive load of managing the work.

### 05 — Laws + Procedures
- Give Naya clear rules for how excellence should be pursued by default.
- Protect approved work, important constraints and decisions from accidental drift.
- Create repeatable procedures instead of relying on memory or improvisation.
- Build verification and quality checks into the way work gets done.
- Make your preferred way of working persistent instead of re-explaining it every time.

### 06 — Scorecarding
- Replace “looks good” with a clear, evidence-based evaluation.
- Measure work against the outcome and criteria that actually matter.
- Expose the biggest weaknesses before they become expensive problems.
- Ask “Why is this not a 10?” and turn the answer into specific improvements.
- Create a repeatable loop: score → improve → verify → score again.

### 07 — Naya Language
- Create a shared vocabulary so you can communicate complex intent quickly.
- Use simple commands that activate proven ways of working.
- Reduce the amount of prompting needed to get the kind of help you want.
- Make your preferred concepts, standards and workflows easier to express.
- Build a collaboration language that becomes more powerful as you learn to use it.

## Content rules

- Always call the note system **Naya Notes**. Never “Smart Notes” and never “9 Notes.”
- Do not present the seven pillars as the complete set of all Naya capabilities or all 18 Masters. They are the seven visitor-facing pillars selected for E06.
- Benefits should answer **what's in it for me?** rather than describing implementation mechanics.
- Keep the Supercharger distinction simple: it makes the existing AI experience more persistent, personalized, proactive, structured and quality-driven.
- Do not introduce pricing in E06. Pricing and the Human Maximus Codex lifetime offer belong later in the conversion sequence.
