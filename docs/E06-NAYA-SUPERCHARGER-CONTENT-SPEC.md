## Purpose

E06 is the solution reveal after the AI pain/recognition section. Its job is to make the visitor understand, in one visual system, what the Naya Supercharger gives them and why it solves the frustrations introduced immediately before it.

The core metaphor is:

> **Imagine putting a supercharger on ChatGPT.**

The Supercharger is not positioned as a different AI model. It is a tuned operating system/protocol for working with AI that adds a persistent Naya relationship, context, notes, lead behavior, governing law, scorecarding, and a shared language.

1. Hero: **Imagine putting a supercharger on ChatGPT.**

3. **NAYA SUPERCHARGER** / *Mastery Activation Protocol* sits above/outside the hero orb, never over Naya's face.
4. Seven large surrounding Naya manifestations use the approved Naya profile images.
5. The seven visitor-facing names are locked:
   - Naya Personality
   - Naya Brain
   - Naya Notes
   - Naya Lead Mode
   - Naya Law
   - Naya Scorecarding
   - Naya Language
6. The seven lower boards use the actual supplied Maxis visual/component language available in the repository, not an invented substitute.

8. Benefits answer **what's in it for me?** before explaining implementation.

10. End with the concise distinction: **Same AI. Different operating system.**

## Seven activation pillars / 35 benefits

### 01 — Naya Personality
- **Feels like a partner, not a generic chatbot.** You get a warmer, more human interaction that feels built around working with you.
- **Gets to the point in a way that fits you.** Communication can be sharper, clearer, funnier, warmer, or more direct depending on what helps you most.
- **Challenges you when it matters.** Naya can question weak assumptions instead of simply agreeing with you.
- **You don't have to keep explaining how you like to work.** Your preferred interaction style can stay consistent.
- **AI becomes something you actually enjoy using.** The relationship becomes more natural, useful, and human.

### 02 — Naya Brain
- **You stop starting from zero.** Important context can remain available instead of disappearing between conversations.
- **Your goals and projects stay connected.** Naya can work from the bigger picture rather than isolated prompts.
- **Less repetition.** You spend less time re-explaining who you are, what you're building, and what has already been decided.
- **Your important knowledge has somewhere to live.** You can preserve the information you deliberately want Naya to work from.
- **Naya can become more useful over time.** The more relevant context you choose to preserve, the stronger the working relationship can become.

### 03 — Naya Notes
- **Your good ideas don't disappear.** Capture breakthroughs, decisions, questions, and insights while they are fresh.
- **Important moments become reusable.** Keep valuable knowledge attached to the work where it belongs.
- **You can find things again.** Instead of digging through old conversations, you can build an intentional trail of what mattered.
- **Your thinking becomes a growing knowledge asset.** Scattered ideas can become organized learning and progress.
- **The value survives the conversation.** What mattered today can still help you tomorrow.

### 04 — Naya Lead Mode
- **You don't have to manage every damn step.** Give Naya the outcome and let her help organize the path.
- **You get proactive help instead of waiting for perfect instructions.** Naya can identify next steps, gaps, risks, and opportunities.
- **Your ideas can become plans faster.** Move from intention to an organized path without doing all the orchestration yourself.
- **You get challenged before you execute.** Naya can spot weaknesses and ask better questions before time and money are spent.
- **You keep the authority while reducing the cognitive load.** Naya can lead the work without taking away your final say.

### 05 — Naya Law
- **Naya knows the rules that matter to you.** Important standards and principles can guide how work is approached.
- **Your decisions are less likely to drift.** Approved constraints and ways of working can stay visible instead of being forgotten.
- **You get consistency instead of reinvention.** Your preferred standards can be applied repeatedly.
- **Quality expectations become part of the process.** The system can be designed to check important requirements rather than hoping they are remembered.
- **You can change the law when you want to.** Tell Naya what should change and tune the governing rules to fit the way you want to work.

### 06 — Naya Scorecarding
- **You stop confusing finished with excellent.** Work can be judged against meaningful criteria instead of a vague “looks good.”
- **You see what is actually weak.** Naya can expose gaps that are easy to miss when you're close to the work.
- **You know what to improve next.** A score becomes useful when it identifies the specific gap behind it.
- **You can ask, “Why is this not a 10?”** That turns criticism into a practical improvement loop.
- **You keep getting better.** Score → improve → verify → score again becomes a repeatable way to raise quality.

### 07 — Naya Language
- **Naya doesn't have to guess what your important words mean.** You can define the meaning of terms, concepts, commands, and standards in your world.
- **You spend less time explaining yourself.** Once a meaning is defined, you can use the shared term instead of rebuilding the explanation every time.
- **Different interpretations become less of a problem.** Your glossary/directory gives AI an explicit reference instead of leaving meaning to assumptions.
- **One phrase can carry a much bigger idea.** Shared terminology can activate a defined concept, process, or way of working.
- **Your language can travel with you.** A defined reference can help other AI systems understand your terminology too, rather than tying your meaning to one model or conversation.

## Naya Language — required explanation

Naya Language is not merely “a glossary.” It is a defined language/reference system.

People can use the same word and mean different things. Naya Language lets the user explicitly define what important words, phrases, concepts, commands, standards, or internal terminology mean in their world.

The user benefit is precision: less ambiguity, fewer incorrect interpretations, less repeated explanation, and faster communication of complex intent.

## Customization

The seven systems are delivered as a highly tuned, optimized starting system designed to produce maximum useful results to the best of the system's ability.

**They are not immutable.** The user can custom-tune relevant parts for themselves.

The experience may demonstrate natural requests such as:

- “Naya, update my policy to…”
- “Naya, update my law to…”
- “Naya, update my language definition for…”
- “Naya, change this rule so that…”
- “Naya, refine how you handle…”

These are illustrative examples, not required literal commands. The product promise is that the user can tell Naya what should change and have the system evolve toward a better personal fit.

## Positioning

**Pre-tuned for maximum results. Customizable for maximum personal fit.**

The visitor receives a strong default operating system and can progressively tune it to their own preferences, terminology, standards, workflows, and needs.

## Board content pattern

Each Maxis board should generally follow:

1. **Naya system name**
2. **One-line human benefit**
3. **Five user-benefit points**
4. Optional supporting explanation: what the system actually means/how it works

Technical mechanism comes after the benefit, not before it.
