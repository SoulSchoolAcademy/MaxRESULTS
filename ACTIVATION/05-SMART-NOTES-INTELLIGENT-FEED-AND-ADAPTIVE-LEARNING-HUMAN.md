# 1. THE SIMPLE IDEA

Naya Power does not just remember what happened.

> **CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → LEARN → ADOPT → COMPOUND**

Naya Power does the work.

---

# 2. ONE SMART NOTE = ONE INTELLIGENCE EVENT

A Smart Note is not a separate note that lives beside the Intelligent Feed.

It is the intelligence event itself.

When something meaningful happens, Naya Power captures **one topic** and expresses that intelligence through the perspectives that make it useful.

A Smart Note contains:

- **BIG TOPIC** — what this intelligence is about
- **DATE / TIME** — when it happened, using only truthful precision
- **TYPE** — insight, decision, lesson, mistake, success, discovery, etc.
- **IN A NUTSHELL** — the most powerful, simplest expression of the intelligence
- **HUMAN** — what the human experienced, meant, decided, or discovered
- **NAYA** — what Naya understood, contributed, or recognized
- **MACHINE** — what the system can objectively observe, structure, or verify
- **CHILD / GRANDMA** — the simplest explanation a person should be able to understand
- **KEY POINTS** — the essential supporting intelligence
- **EVIDENCE** — what proves or supports the important claims
- **SMART LINK** — the durable connection to evidence, related intelligence, learning, and future use

The four perspectives are not four separate notes.
They are four views of the **same intelligence event**.

When you press **POST**, you are not creating a Smart Note in one place and then separately posting something to a feed.

You are publishing the canonical intelligence event into the intelligence system.

The flow is:

**SMART NOTE**
↓
**INTELLIGENCE EVENT**
↓
**ACTIVITY INTELLIGENCE FEED**
↓
**HUB INTELLIGENT FEED**

# 4. THEN ADAPTIVE LEARNING LOOKS AT IT

Once the intelligence event exists, Naya Power asks:

> **Is there something here that should make the system smarter next time?**

Not every Smart Note becomes a rule.

A personal moment, ordinary update, or simple observation may simply remain useful memory.

But a mistake, correction, successful pattern, safeguard, decision, discovery, or reusable lesson may contain operational intelligence.

The learning path is:

**INTELLIGENCE EVENT**
→ **LEARNING EVENT**
→ **LESSON**
→ **EVIDENCE / VERIFICATION**
→ **RULE OR PATTERN**
→ **PREFLIGHT**
→ **BETTER FUTURE ACTION**

This is where memory becomes learning.

# 7. LEARNING MUST BE EARNED

Naya Power does not blindly turn every observation into a permanent rule.

Learning can move through:

**OBSERVED → PROPOSED → CONFIRMED → OPERATIONAL → SUPERSEDED**

A lesson becomes operational only when the evidence and governance appropriate to that lesson support it.

This protects the system from learning the wrong thing too quickly.
