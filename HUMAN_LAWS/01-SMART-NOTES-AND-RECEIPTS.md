# NAYA POWER — LAW 01
## SMART NOTES + RECEIPTS
### The Verified Intelligence Capture Law

**Status:** OFFICIAL HUMAN LAW GUIDE  
**Level:** Foundational / Mandatory  
**Effective:** 2026-09-05  
**Applies to:** Every human using Naya Power and every Naya experience implementing Naya Power

---

## 1. The Promise

When something matters, Naya should capture the **actual intelligence** — not merely a description of the conversation — and prove that it was captured.

A Smart Note is therefore not simply a note. It is a **verified intelligence-capture event**.

The standard is simple:

> **If it matters, capture it. If you say it was captured, prove it.**

---

## 2. What Smart Note Means

A Smart Note preserves useful intelligence such as an:

- insight
- lesson
- mistake
- breakthrough
- decision
- idea
- question
- goal
- opportunity
- win
- important realization
- meaningful change in direction

The purpose is to turn a moment of understanding into durable, usable intelligence that can compound over time.

A Smart Note is **not** a transcript, a generic summary, or a note about the fact that a conversation happened.

---

## 3. Natural Language Counts

The human should not have to learn a secret command language.

If the human says things such as:

- “Make a Smart Note of this.”
- “Make a note of this.”
- “Hey Naya, note that.”
- “Capture this.”
- “Remember this.”
- “Save that.”
- “Keep that.”

Naya should recognize the preservation intent when the surrounding context indicates meaningful intelligence.

The human does **not** need to say the exact words “Smart Note.”

---

## 4. Identify the Subject First

Before capturing the intelligence, Naya identifies **what the intelligence is about**.

The subject is the anchor that makes the intelligence understandable, retrievable, verifiable, and useful later.

Examples:

- NayaNET Intelligent Hub architecture
- a product decision
- a customer insight
- a personal lesson
- a project blocker
- a technical discovery

If the subject is genuinely ambiguous, Naya should resolve the ambiguity rather than silently attaching the intelligence to the wrong thing.

---

## 5. The Four-Artifact Model

A verified Smart Note produces four distinct representations:

### 1. Human Note
What the human experienced, decided, learned, noticed, or wants preserved.

### 2. Naya Note
What Naya understands to be the deeper intelligence, implication, pattern, or lesson.

### 3. Machine Note
Structured information that allows software to reliably store, retrieve, process, connect, and verify the intelligence.

### 4. Intelligence Feed
The durable intelligence event that can participate in personal intelligence, reporting, compounding intelligence, and — when authorized — the Intelligent Hub and collective systems.

These four representations serve different purposes. They are not four copies of the same paragraph.

---

## 6. The Required Chain

A complete Smart Note follows this chain:

**REQUEST → IDENTIFY SUBJECT → EXTRACT INTELLIGENCE → HUMAN NOTE → NAYA NOTE → MACHINE NOTE → INTELLIGENCE FEED → VERIFY → RECEIPT → INTELLIGENT HUB**

The chain is designed to prevent a common failure: Naya saying “I remembered that” when the system never actually created durable evidence.

---

## 7. Understanding Is Not Recording

This distinction is fundamental.

> **Naya understanding something is not the same as the system recording it.**

A conversation can be understood perfectly and still fail to produce durable intelligence.

Likewise, a database row can exist without containing meaningful intelligence.

The Smart Note contract requires both:

**meaningful intelligence + verifiable persistence**.

---

## 8. The Receipt Is Proof

The receipt is the human-readable proof that the Smart Note was actually completed.

A receipt should identify, as applicable:

- Smart Note / event ID
- subject
- status
- timestamp
- Human Note
- Naya Note
- Machine Note
- Intelligence Feed
- provenance/source
- privacy and sharing state
- verification state
- relevant artifact links or identifiers

The receipt should allow a human or authorized system to answer:

> **What happened, where is it, and how do we know it really happened?**

---

## 9. The Hard Completion Rule

### NO FOUR VERIFIED ARTIFACTS = NO RECEIPT

### NO VALID LINKS OR EVIDENCE = NO VERIFIED COMPLETION

### NO VERIFIED COMPLETION = NAYA MUST NOT CLAIM COMPLETION

Naya must never manufacture a receipt simply because the desired result sounds plausible.

No fabricated:

- IDs
- URLs
- timestamps
- hashes
- commit references
- verification results
- database records
- completion claims

---

## 10. Failure Is Visible

If the storage system, connector, runtime, database, or other required component is unavailable, Naya must say so.

The correct behavior is not silent failure.

The correct behavior is:

**detect → explain → preserve what is safe to preserve → identify what failed → provide the next action → retry or complete when authorized → verify**

A failed capture must never be represented as a successful capture.

---

## 11. Privacy

The governing privacy principle is:

> **Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

Creating a Smart Note does not automatically publish it.

Private intelligence remains private unless an explicit authorized action changes its sharing state.

---

## 12. The Intelligent Hub Relationship

The Intelligent Hub is the human-facing control center and evidence layer for NayaNET intelligence.

Relevant verified Smart Note events should be available to the Hub according to authorization and privacy rules.

The Hub may surface:

- recent Smart Notes
- intelligence feed entries
- Daily Intelligence Briefings
- Weekly Intelligence Reports
- longer-term intelligence
- evidence and receipts
- project continuity
- connection/source status
- authorized Intelligent Blocks
- collective intelligence resulting from permitted sharing

The Hub must not imply that an item is live merely because a document says it should be integrated. Runtime integration must be implemented and verified.

---

## 13. Smart Notes and Naya Notes

Naya Notes are part of the broader durable memory/context system.

A Smart Note is more specific: it is a **verified intelligence-capture event and contract**.

Therefore:

**Naya Notes = durable memory/context capability.**  
**Smart Notes = verified intelligence events that can feed that capability and the wider intelligence system.**

---

## 14. Compounding Intelligence

A single Smart Note can become useful far beyond the moment in which it was created.

Over time, verified events can support:

**Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 7 → Day 30 → Day 365 → Lifetime**

They can reveal:

- recurring patterns
- repeated mistakes
- successful decisions
- changing priorities
- emerging opportunities
- improvements in capability
- project momentum
- personal growth
- system learning

The point is not to collect more notes. The point is to create **better intelligence over time**.

---

## 15. Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Yearly Intelligence

Verified intelligence events provide the evidence base for derived reporting.

A Daily Intelligence Briefing should tell the human what matters now.

A Weekly Intelligence Report should explain what happened, what changed, what was learned, what remains unresolved, and what should happen next.

Monthly and yearly intelligence should reveal longer patterns and compounding change.

Reports must be based on actual durable evidence. Naya must not fabricate a historical period that was never recorded.

---

## 16. The Intelligent Block Boundary

A Smart Note does not automatically become collective intelligence.

The progression is:

**Personal Intelligence → Sharing Decision → Authorized Intelligent Block → NayaNET Collective**

Only authorized intelligence crosses the sharing boundary.

---

## 17. What the Human Should Experience

The experience should feel simple:

1. The human says something meaningful.
2. The human asks Naya to capture it — naturally.
3. Naya identifies the subject and extracts the intelligence.
4. The system records the required artifacts.
5. The system verifies them.
6. Naya gives a real receipt.
7. The intelligence becomes available to the appropriate durable systems.
8. The human can move on without wondering whether it was actually saved.

---

## 18. What Good Looks Like

A successful Smart Note is:

- meaningful
- correctly attributed
- durable
- structured
- traceable
- privacy-aware
- verifiable
- reusable
- connected to future intelligence

---

## 19. The Human Contract

When you ask Naya to remember something important, you should be able to expect:

> **Naya will understand what matters, capture the actual intelligence, protect it according to your sharing choice, verify the capture, and show you evidence that it happened.**

If Naya cannot complete that contract, Naya should tell you rather than pretend.
