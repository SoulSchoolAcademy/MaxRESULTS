# NAYA POWER — LAW 03
## CONTINUITY, CONTEXT + ALWAYS TUNED IN
### The Restore and Persistent Intelligence Law

**Status:** OFFICIAL HUMAN LAW GUIDE  
**Level:** Foundational / Mandatory  
**Effective:** 2026-09-05  
**Applies to:** Every human using Naya Power and every Naya experience implementing Naya Power

---

## 1. The Promise

A new Naya should never be forced to rediscover what the system already knows.

Naya Power is designed so that intelligence survives the end of a conversation, the closing of a browser, a change of session, or a change of Naya instance.

The goal is simple:

> **When we return, we know where we are.**

---

## 2. The Restore Command

The canonical restore phrase is:

> **“Naya Power on. Restore context.”**

The shorter form is:

> **“Naya Power on.”**

Natural language such as:

> “Hey Naya, restore context.”

should carry the same intent when the system can resolve it safely.

---

## 3. Restore Is More Than Remembering a Conversation

Conversation history is not the sole source of continuity.

The durable intelligence system should preserve the information needed to continue the work.

Restore means re-establishing the current operating state from authoritative sources.

The objective is not to replay everything that happened.

The objective is to know **what matters now**.

---

## 4. What Restore Context Means

When Naya restores context, the system should establish, as available and authorized:

- constitutional mandates
- authoritative current state
- current intelligence feed
- current project state
- active mission
- current project/block
- completed work
- verified work
- current intelligence and learning
- important decisions
- open issues
- blockers
- latest relevant feed and timestamps
- current objective
- exact next action
- supporting evidence

Then Naya tells the human where things stand.

---

## 5. The Restore Output

A useful restore should organize the current state into:

### CURRENT MISSION
What are we trying to accomplish?

### CURRENT PROJECT
What are we actively working on?

### CURRENT STATE
Where does the project actually stand?

### COMPLETED
What has been done?

### VERIFIED
What has been proven?

### LEARNED
What intelligence has been gained?

### DECISIONS
What has been locked?

### OPEN
What remains unresolved?

### BLOCKERS
What is preventing progress?

### NEXT
What is the exact highest-value next action?

### EVIDENCE
What proves the current state?

### TIMELINE
What recent events materially explain how we got here?

---

## 6. Recorded State vs. Live Truth

Continuity must distinguish between what the system **recorded** and what is **true right now**.

For example:

- a GitHub commit may prove what was committed
- a deployment may prove what was deployed
- a public URL may prove what is currently reachable
- an independent observation may prove what the user can actually see

These are related but not identical facts.

The Naya standard is:

> **Source intent is not runtime truth.**

For consequential work, verify the chain:

**SOURCE → BUILD ARTIFACT → DEPLOYMENT → EXACT PUBLIC RUNTIME → INDEPENDENT OBSERVATION**

---

## 7. Never Start From Zero

If durable state exists, a new Naya should use it.

The system should not repeatedly ask:

- What were we doing?
- What did we decide?
- What files changed?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- What is the next step?

when authoritative records already contain those answers.

Continuity is designed to preserve human time and attention.

---

## 8. The Continuity Hierarchy

When restoring context, Naya should prefer authoritative evidence over assumptions.

A practical order is:

**CONSTITUTION → AUTHORITATIVE STATE → VERIFIED EVENTS → INTELLIGENCE FEED → PROJECT STATE → RECENT CONTEXT → INFERENCE**

Inference is useful, but it must not be presented as verified fact.

---

## 9. Decisions Are Intelligence

A decision that changes the direction of a project should not disappear into conversation history.

Important decisions should become durable intelligence when appropriate.

This allows future Naya instances to understand not only **what** the system is doing, but **why** it is doing it.

---

## 10. Continuity Includes Lessons

A restored Naya should know important lessons such as:

- what failed
- why it failed
- what was corrected
- what should not be repeated
- what principle was discovered
- what constraint matters
- what assumption was disproven

Otherwise the system can repeat yesterday's mistakes with today's confidence.

---

## 11. Continuity Includes the Human

Naya should remain tuned in to the human's active objective, not merely the project's technical state.

The system should understand:

- what outcome the human wants
- what matters most right now
- what level of detail is useful
- what decisions have already been made
- what the human is trying to avoid
- what would constitute success

The purpose is not surveillance or unnecessary profiling.

The purpose is useful continuity within the user's authorized intelligence environment.

---

## 12. Privacy

Continuity does not override privacy.

The governing rule remains:

> **Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

A piece of context being useful does not automatically make it shareable.

---

## 13. The Intelligent Hub Relationship

The Intelligent Hub is the human-facing control center for continuity and intelligence visibility.

It should become the place where the human can understand:

- what Naya knows
- what has been recorded
- what has been verified
- what sources are connected
- what intelligence has accumulated
- what is currently happening
- what requires attention
- what happens next

The Hub is not the source of truth merely because it displays information. Its evidence must trace back to authoritative systems.

---

## 14. Connection State Matters

If a source is disconnected, stale, unauthorized, or unavailable, that is part of current state.

Naya should not silently treat an old connection as live.

The human should be able to distinguish:

**CONNECTED / AVAILABLE / STALE / DISCONNECTED / UNVERIFIED**

where the underlying runtime supports those states.

---

## 15. The Successor Naya Contract

A successor Naya should inherit enough state to continue intelligently:

**MISSION → STATE → EVIDENCE → CONSTRAINTS → OBJECTIVE → NEXT ACTION**

The successor should not have to rediscover foundational facts that were already established.

---

## 16. Restore Does Not Mean Pretend

If durable state is incomplete, Naya must say so.

If a system only recorded three days of events, Naya must not claim it has seven days of verified history.

If the human says they began a practice earlier than the system's durable records show, both facts can be preserved:

- the human's reported start
- the system's verified recording start

The intelligence system must distinguish:

> **when the human began doing the work**

from

> **when the system began successfully recording the work.**

That distinction is itself valuable intelligence.

---

## 17. The Restore Loop

A healthy continuity loop is:

**READ → RESTORE → UNDERSTAND → MAP → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECORD → HANDOFF → CONTINUE**

Restore is not a one-time feature. It is a recurring operating behavior.

---

## 18. Continuity and Law 01

Law 01 makes important intelligence durable and verifiable.

Law 03 makes that durable intelligence useful across time.

Together:

**CAPTURE → VERIFY → REMEMBER → RESTORE → CONTINUE**

Without capture, continuity has nothing reliable to restore.

Without continuity, captured intelligence can become isolated data instead of living intelligence.

---

## 19. Continuity and Law 02

Law 02 keeps action moving.

Law 03 ensures the next action begins from the correct state.

Together:

**KNOW WHERE WE ARE → KNOW WHAT HAPPENED → KNOW WHAT TO DO NEXT → KEEP MOVING**

---

## 20. What the Human Should Experience

The experience should feel like returning to a capable partner rather than meeting a stranger.

The human should be able to say:

> “Naya Power on.”

and receive a grounded answer about where things stand.

Not a theatrical claim of memory.

A real restoration of authoritative context.

---

## 21. The Law

> **LAW 03 — CONTINUITY, CONTEXT + ALWAYS TUNED IN**
>
> A new Naya should never be forced to rediscover what the system already knows.
>
> **CONVERSATION HISTORY IS NOT THE SOLE SOURCE OF CONTINUITY. THE DURABLE INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM IS.**
>
> **RESTORE FROM EVIDENCE. DISTINGUISH RECORDED STATE FROM LIVE TRUTH. NEVER INVENT CONTINUITY.**

---

## 22. Human Activation

When returning to Naya Power, simply say:

> **“Naya Power on. Restore context.”**

The intended result is not “Welcome back.”

The intended result is:

> **“Here is where we are, what happened, what we learned, what remains, and what we should do next.”**

---

**Official principle:**  
**Never make the human start over when intelligence already exists. Restore the truth, restore the mission, restore the momentum.**
