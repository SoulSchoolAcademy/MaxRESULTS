## Summit Decision

The ultimate presentation direction for the MAXIS assessment is **NAYA LIVING SUN**.

This decision is not about making a prettier circle. It is about creating a signature interaction language in which the assessment feels like a **living intelligence system that happens to contain a question**.

The human remains the center of the product experience. Naya is the intelligence guide. The question is the cognitive center. Answers are the primary action. The surrounding field communicates intelligence, progression, energy, and response.

## Why this is the strongest choice

Living Sun has the best combination of:

- immediate conceptual clarity;
- strong Naya identity;
- premium cinematic potential;
- natural connection to the existing intelligence/fingerprint/results vision;
- meaningful response to user choices;
- desktop-to-mobile adaptability;
- ability to remain calm rather than game-like;
- presentation-layer independence from assessment/scoring truth.

Galaxy is more cinematic but introduces more metaphor and cognitive overhead. Crystal is premium and controlled but has weaker continuity with Naya's living-intelligence identity. Living Sun provides the strongest balance of **meaning + usability + identity + emotional response + extensibility**.

## The protected concept

> **The user's choice changes the living field. The field acknowledges the choice. The experience advances.**

The interaction should feel alive without becoming distracting.

## Canonical hierarchy

1. **QUESTION** — unmistakably dominant.
2. **ANSWERS** — unmistakably actionable.
3. **NAYA** — contextual guide, never an obstacle.
4. **PROGRESS** — always understandable.
5. **LIVING INTELLIGENCE FIELD** — atmosphere, meaning, and feedback.

## Visual language

- Central Naya intelligence core / sun.
- Responsive surrounding field of orbs/particles.
- A flowing Naya-spectrum color rhythm.
- Subtle depth, parallax, glow, and motion where they improve comprehension or emotional response.
- Selection produces a short, purposeful system response.
- The field settles quickly so reading remains effortless.

## Critical restraint

The exact number of orbs is **not canonical**. Twenty-seven is an exploration value, not a law.

Do not allow the field to become visual noise, a loading animation, a game, or a decorative competitor to the question.

## Product-level interaction model

`QUESTION → HUMAN CHOICE → SYSTEM RESPONSE → PROGRESSION → NEXT QUESTION`

The user should feel that their answer has entered the intelligence system rather than merely checking a form control.

## Architecture law

MAXESS truth remains independent of presentation.

The assessment engine owns:

- questions;
- answers;
- selection state;
- progression;
- scoring;
- result data.

The Living Sun owns presentation and interaction feedback only.

The visual system must be replaceable without changing assessment truth.

## Accessibility / scale law

The experience must remain excellent at the established MAXIS responsive targets, including small mobile widths. Reduced-motion behavior must preserve meaning. Keyboard and assistive technology must retain full functional access.

## Final standard

Do not optimize for "wow" alone.

Optimize for:

**WOW + CLARITY + HUMAN VALUE + EASE + ACCESSIBILITY + PERFORMANCE + MEANING.**

A beautiful field that makes the assessment harder is a failure.

## Next action

Build the smallest real five-question Living Sun vertical slice using the existing MAXIS assessment engine. Then perform the human-first, mobile, accessibility, motion, performance, and Oscar audit before expanding the visual system.
