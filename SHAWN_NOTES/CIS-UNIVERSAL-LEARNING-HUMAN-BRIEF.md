## What is CIS?

**CIS = Compounding Intelligence System.**

It is not just a notebook.

Think of it like this:

**Today you learn something → you keep the important part → you understand it better → you connect it to what you already know → you use it → you learn from the result → tomorrow starts smarter than today.**

The goal is not to save every word forever. The goal is to keep what matters and make it useful.

### The CIS loop

**EXPERIENCE → CAPTURE → DISTILL → CONNECT → VERIFY → COMPOUND → APPLY → LEARN AGAIN**

Over time:

**DAY → WEEK → MONTH → YEAR → LIFETIME**

That is the compounding effect.

## How Naya and the notes work

There can be two useful versions of an important note.

### Naya Note
Written for an AI: compact, structured, precise, terminology-locked, and easy for Naya to retrieve and use later.

### Shawn / Human Note
Written for people: simple, clear, easy to scan, and understandable without having to reread the original conversation.

When an important conversation produces something worth keeping, Naya can suggest making a note. The human decides whether to save it. It should be intentional, not automatic just because something was said.

When a note is created, Naya should return the link so the person can verify what was actually saved.

## Where the notes can live

There are three basic paths:

**A — Naya does it:** “Naya, make a note.” She creates it and returns the verification link.

**B — GitHub:** the person can create a file directly.

**C — Google Drive:** the person can connect Drive and use it for documents and shared information.

GitHub is the preferred Naya Power storage path because it provides strong history, versioning, organization, traceability, and development capabilities. Google Drive is especially convenient for people who already use Drive every day.

The important distinction is:

> **GitHub is storage. CIS is the intelligence system.**

CIS should not depend on one storage provider.

## MAXESS: prove what you learned

This is where the pieces connect.

Imagine:

**1. Ask Naya to teach you something.**

**2. Learn and practice.**

**3. Tell MAXESS what you learned.**

**4. MAXESS creates an assessment for that subject.**

**5. You take the test.**

**6. You receive a score and a level.**

**7. You see what you know and what you need to improve.**

**8. You can learn more and take it again.**

**9. Your progress can become a report and, where appropriate, a certificate/recognition of achievement.**

So the loop becomes:

**LEARN → PRACTICE → ASSESS → SCORE → LEARN MORE → RETAKE → IMPROVE → RECOGNIZE → REMEMBER → COMPOUND**

The assessment is not there to punish people. It is there to show them where they are and give them a way to grow.

## Why the certificate matters

The certificate is not supposed to pretend to be a university degree or government accreditation.

Its purpose is simpler and more human:

> **You learned something. You challenged yourself. You measured yourself. You improved. You achieved something worth recognizing.**

A future certificate can include the subject, score/level, date, learner, Naya Power Academy identity, and a unique identifier or verification mechanism.

The most important credential is still the knowledge itself.
