# SMART NODE — NayaNET Intelligent Hub / Living Sun

**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Event:** `NAYANET-INTELLIGENT-HUB-LIVING-SUN-CONCEPT-2026-08-31`

## Canonical learning

The Intelligent Hub is a central operating environment, not a dashboard. Its primary conceptual model is a Living Sun: Naya at the center, network destinations orbiting as intelligent nodes.

## Architectural truths

1. Front Door and Hub are one continuous experience.
2. Name-only entry is the intended low-friction threshold.
3. Naya is the center of the Hub experience.
4. Voice-first interaction is preferred: TALK TO NAYA / LISTEN TO NAYA.
5. A full typed interface is an expansion mode.
6. The five identity jewels are persistent network vocabulary.
7. Inner and outer orbital layers provide extensibility.
8. The visual system must communicate premium depth without becoming cluttered.
9. The Hub must route to real worlds and must never imply unfinished functionality is live.
10. The design is not frozen until Shawn reviews the actual implementation.

## Success signal

The experience should make a first-time user feel that they entered a private intelligent network rather than opened another website.
