## What Naya understands now

NayaNET is not being presented as another collection of tools. The Intelligent Hub is the human-facing doorway into a private new internet / intelligent network where Naya connects the person to identity, intelligence, learning, creation and future network capabilities.

The Hub should make a person feel that something meaningful has happened immediately after entering their name. The emotional transition is:

**You're in. This is yours.**

The five identity jewels are the first persistent visual language of the network:

- Smart Name
- Smart ID
- Smart Link
- Smart Mail
- Naya Power

Their visual order is Gold → Green → Sapphire → Indigo → Magenta. They are dimensional jewels/orbs, not flat dashboard icons.

Naya Power is the preferred next path. MAXIS is a second major path. Other ecosystem areas are visible as part of the network vision but must not be represented as completed functionality until their authoritative implementations and URLs exist.

Future Naya successors should preserve the Hub's premium visual language and should not regress into a generic dashboard, flat text cards, low-contrast UI or invented integrations.
