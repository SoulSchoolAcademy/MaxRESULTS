# NOTE EVENT — NayaNET Intelligent Hub Living Sun

**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Event:** `NAYANET-INTELLIGENT-HUB-LIVING-SUN-CONCEPT-2026-08-31`  
**Status:** CURRENT DESIGN CONCEPT / PENDING VISUAL REVIEW

## Decision

Shawn and Naya agreed to evolve the NayaNET Front Door + Intelligent Hub into one continuous premium environment centered on a Living Sun representation of Naya.

## Core idea

The user enters NayaNET with name-only friction, then transitions into an Intelligent Hub where Naya is at the center and NayaNET destinations form a living orbital constellation around her.

## Key design decisions

- No conventional SaaS dashboard.
- Front Door and Hub feel like one environment.
- Central Living Sun / Naya intelligence orb.
- Primary voice controls: TALK TO NAYA and LISTEN TO NAYA.
- Optional expanded typed interface.
- Inner orbit for primary destinations; outer orbit reserved for future network growth.
- Persistent bottom identity rail using SMART NAME, SMART ID, SMART LINK, SMART MAIL and NAYA POWER.
- Canonical identity colors: Gold, Green, Sapphire, Indigo, Magenta.
- Academy remains the visual-quality reference and is not redesigned.
- Motion must communicate living depth, not become spectacle.
- Live functionality must be truthful.

## Design principle

Treat Shawn's ideas as seeds. Preserve intent, then expand them into a more profound, elegant and useful experience rather than implementing them literally or minimally.

## Freeze status

This is a master concept and operating direction. It is intentionally **not** a final visual freeze until Shawn sees and reviews the implemented experience.

## Next move

Build the Front Door → Living Sun Hub → identity rail → Naya Power/MAXIS orbital vertical slice, verify it, then refine before expanding the network.
