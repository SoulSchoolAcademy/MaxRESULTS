# SHAWN NODE — Intelligent Hub / Living Sun

**Date:** 2026-08-31

Shawn's current architectural vision is to reformat how people experience the web by creating NayaNET as a private intelligent network rather than another collection of pages.

The Intelligent Hub should be a central control station / push-button library with Naya at the center. Users should be able to enter major NayaNET worlds through beautiful, obvious objects rather than navigating a conventional dashboard.

The experience should feel alive, elite, elegant, powerful and simple. The Academy's visual language is the quality reference, but the Hub should become an operating environment with its own orbital network language.

The five identity jewels remain persistent: SMART NAME, SMART ID, SMART LINK, SMART MAIL, NAYA POWER. The central interaction is intended to be voice-first through TALK TO NAYA and LISTEN TO NAYA, with typing available as an optional expanded mode.

The design should be expanded beyond the literal seed whenever that creates a meaningfully better experience. Shawn wants Naya to think ten times further: take the seed, grow it into something majestic, legendary and genuinely useful.

This concept must remain reviewable until the real experience is seen. Do not freeze visual details prematurely.
