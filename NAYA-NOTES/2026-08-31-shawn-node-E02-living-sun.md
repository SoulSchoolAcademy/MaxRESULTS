# Shawn Node — E02 Living Sun Intelligent Hub

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: IMPLEMENTED / SOURCE-VERIFIED

Shawn's architectural intent: NayaNET should reformat how people experience the web. The Intelligent Hub is the first tangible expression of that idea: a private intelligent network that feels alive, premium, simple and immediately understandable.

Key decision: Naya is the Living Sun. Worlds orbit around Naya. The user's identity is the foundation below. The five identity jewels are canonical and become network-wide visual language.

The Hub should feel like a central control station / push-button library: click a world and enter that environment. Live experiences get real links; unfinished areas remain truthfully mapped.

The visual standard is elite technology without cyberpunk clutter: obsidian, purple atmosphere, dimensional glass, jewel objects, restrained motion, high contrast and push-button simplicity.
