# Intelligence Activity Feed — What Smart Notes Are and How They Work

**New intelligence recorded:** The Smart Note is the canonical four-perspective capture unit.

A single Smart Note event produces Human, Naya, Machine, and Intelligence Activity Feed representations. The four artifacts remain linked to one event ID, one timestamp, one privacy state, and one verification lifecycle.

**Compounding signal:** New knowledge has entered the personal intelligence layer and is available for chronological retrieval and later Daily Intelligence Report synthesis.
